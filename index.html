About Us

WebIsForGames is a platform dedicated to showcasing innovative web-based games and interactive experiences. We believe that the web is an incredible platform for gaming, offering accessibility, cross-platform compatibility, and instant playability.

🎮

Web-First Gaming

All our games are built with web technologies, ensuring they work seamlessly across devices without downloads.

🚀

Instant Play

No installation required. Click and play instantly from any modern web browser.

🌐

Cross-Platform

Works on desktop, mobile, and tablet devices. One codebase, everywhere.

⚡

Modern Tech

Built with cutting-edge web technologies including WebGL, Canvas API, and Web Audio API.

Our Mission

To demonstrate the power and potential of web technologies in game development, making games more accessible and enjoyable for everyone.
